Your Dedicated Uniform & Clothing Partner

At UNISTYLE UNIFORMS, we design and produce premium clothing and uniform collections that embody your brand's prestige, unite your workforce, and endure daily professional demands.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our uniform services.

Our minimum order quantity varies by product type. For standard uniforms, we accept orders starting from 20 pieces. Custom-designed uniforms require a minimum of 50 pieces. Contact us for specific requirements and we'll find the best solution for your team size.

Standard orders are typically completed within 2-3 weeks. Custom-designed uniforms take 3-5 weeks depending on complexity. Rush orders can be accommodated within 7-10 business days for an additional fee. We always provide a detailed timeline during consultation.

Absolutely! We offer multiple branding options including embroidery, screen printing, sublimation printing, and woven labels. Our design team will work with you to ensure your logo and branding elements are perfectly represented on every uniform.

Yes! We provide sample uniforms for approval before proceeding with bulk production. This ensures fit, fabric quality, and design meet your expectations. Sample costs are typically deducted from the bulk order price upon confirmation.

We offer a comprehensive size range from XS to 5XL for most products. Custom sizing is also available for teams with specific requirements. We can arrange on-site measuring sessions for large orders to ensure perfect fit for every team member.

We offer a 30-day exchange policy for sizing issues. Defective items are replaced at no charge. Custom-designed uniforms are final sale, but we ensure quality through our sample approval process. Our after-sales team is always ready to help with any concerns.
